About

Motor design, gearboxes, and control systems. Taipei, Taiwan.

01The short version

I’m 16, entering my junior year at Taipei American School. I work on brushless motor design, gearbox machining, and the control firmware that drives them. I have built FRC robots since 2025, with team 8503 as a rookie and team 4253 since. In 2025 I led the dry lab for GEMS-Taiwan’s iGEM team, which took a gold medal and a top-ten finish.

The common thread across these projects is modeling a system first, then measuring the built version against the model.

02Currently

Building a scaled wheeled-legged robot and its balance controller. Advising the 2026 GEMS-Taiwan iGEM team. Third FRC season overall, second with team 4253, and a four-person RoboCup Junior team.
