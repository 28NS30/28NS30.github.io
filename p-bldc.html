Brushless DC motor

A hand-wound outrunner designed for a torque density not available off the shelf at this diameter. Three revisions, and a brake dyno built to characterise it.

01Why build it

The gearbox I was designing needed roughly [N] N·m continuous at the output inside a [N] mm envelope. Off-the-shelf hobby outrunners in that diameter are wound for RPM, not torque: they hit the speed easily and stall well under the load I needed. The options were a much larger motor and a heavier reduction, or winding for a lower Kv and keeping the package.

I built one in order to measure the trade-off directly.
